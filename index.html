About Kedar Buildcon

Construction built on precision & a promise kept

Kedar Buildcon is a Pune-based construction and development company built by engineers who believe a home or workplace should be as solid in build as it is in design. From the first site survey to the final handover, our in-house team manages structural work, interiors and finishing under one roof.

We've partnered with families, businesses and investors across Pune to deliver spaces that are structurally sound, thoughtfully planned, and completed within committed timelines.
